## 3. Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

```text
Step into a quiet, classic draughts club on your phone or tablet. OpenCheckers brings four world draughts and checkers variants together with six club AI opponents, full-game computer analysis, tactical puzzles, and deep visual and sound customization—all working 100% offline with no ads, no accounts, and no tracking.

FOUR DRAUGHTS & CHECKERS VARIANTS
Play your favorite rule set on an 8×8 or 10×10 board:
• English Draughts / American Checkers (8×8) — Men move and capture forward; kings move one square in either direction.
• International Draughts (10×10) — Twenty pieces each, backward men captures, flying kings, and compulsory maximum capture.
• Russian Draughts (8×8) — Flying kings and immediate mid-capture crowning when a man reaches the far rank during a jump.
• Brazilian Draughts (8×8) — International rules (flying kings and compulsory maximum capture) on a compact 8×8 board.

SIX CLUB AI OPPONENTS OR 2-PLAYER PASS & PLAY
Track your rating and head-to-head record against six distinct club personalities:
• Pip (~400 Elo · The newcomer) — Curious and impulsive; always ready for one more game.
• Cleo (~800 Elo · The café regular) — Easygoing, adventurous, and fond of a cheeky capture.
• Jasper (~1200 Elo · The club captain) — A friendly rival who makes you earn the center.
• Vera (~1600 Elo · The tactician) — Patient and observant; leave a gap and she will find it.
• Orion (~2000 Elo · The strategist) — Calm, calculating, and always looking a few moves ahead.
• Atlas (~2400 Elo · The master) — Quiet confidence where every tempo deserves your attention.

Or choose 2 Players (Pass & Play) on the same device with Untimed, 3 min (Blitz), 5 min (Rapid), or 10 min (Classical) time controls.

FULL-GAME COMPUTER ANALYSIS & SAVED LIBRARY
Study your completed games or imported PDN records:
• Move-by-Move Engine Evaluation: Run full-game computer analysis with an interactive evaluation graph that lets you jump to any ply.
• Variations & Sandbox Play: Explore alternative lines on the analysis board while preserving the main game plot, and switch freely between the Main line and Variations.
• Saved Analysis Library: Save and resume up to 5 full-game computer analyses across app restarts.
• PDN Import & Export: Copy, paste, and import Portable Draughts Notation (PDN) games or set up fresh position boards.

THE PRACTICE ROOM: 37 TACTICAL PUZZLES
Train your eye across 5 difficulty levels (First combinations, Tactical vision, Think ahead, Deep calculation, and Master class):
• 33 verified tactical puzzles from the MIT-licensed Shadow Checkers collection plus 4 original variant lessons.
• Filter by variant (English, International, Russian, Brazilian), request hints, or step through the full solution line.

PERSONALIZE YOUR CLUB
• Board Themes & Finishes: Choose Sage, Walnut, Slate, or Tournament palettes, plus Wood, Linen, Marble, Dots, or Royal textures.
• Custom Photos & Colors: Customize room backgrounds, board squares, and Ivory/Coral pieces—or import your own JPG, PNG, or WebP images.
• Custom Soundscape & Haptics: Built-in tactile audio and haptics for selection, move, capture, promotion, start, finish, victory, and undo—plus support for importing your own MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, or AAC sounds.
• 100% Offline & Private: Every game, rating, analysis, and setting stays on your device. No internet connection or account required.
```
